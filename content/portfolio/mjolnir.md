Mjölnir Games was a game development studio I co-founded with some friends during my studies at Blekinge Institute of Technology. We started small and launched a mobile game called Way of the Knight, built in Unity using C#.
<!--more-->

My official title was programmer, but I mainly focused on quality assurance for the gameplay levels in Way of the Knight for smartphones and tablets. Our primary goal was to develop games for PC and consoles, but we founded the company towards the end of our fourth year at college. With the fifth year devoted to master's thesis work, we did not have time to fully dedicate ourselves to the studio, so we started with a mobile game.

Unfortunately, our different personal situations made it difficult to continue developing the studio. Some of us had found employment elsewhere, some had courses to catch up on, while others were moving back home. Most of the developers decided to leave, and I was one of them.

Way of the Knight did not really pick up, and the original download page is no longer available.
