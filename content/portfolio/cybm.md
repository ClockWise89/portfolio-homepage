Together with five others I co-founded CYBM, where we worked on a social platform focused on exercise challenges and fitness. My role was in development and project management, including developing the iOS app with one of the others. We used Swift and Java for the mobile apps.
<!--more-->

This was an after-work project alongside my full-time employment. My involvement ran from February 2017 to March 2018, overlapping my first period at HiQ and my move to Sleep Cycle.

<img class="centered" src="../img/onewin/fitnessfestivalen.png" alt="OneWin at Fitnessfestivalen" />

At CYBM we worked on a service where users could create, join and watch recorded responses to exercise challenges and share them with the world. We called the service OneWin and launched apps on the App Store and Google Play. Unfortunately, the project did not gain the traction needed to sustain its costs. We decided to close the project and removed the apps from their respective stores. At that point I left the company to focus on other projects.
