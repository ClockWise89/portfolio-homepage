I worked at Sleep Cycle AB as an iOS & Android Native Developer from August 2017 to August 2023. As the company name suggests, its focus is sleep, with an app used by millions across Android and iOS.
<!--more-->

During my first year at Sleep Cycle I worked on their time-tracking app, Life Cycle, for iOS. The app tracks how and where you spend your time and presents it in readable categories and charts. You can see how many hours a day, week, month or year you spend at work, commuting, having lunch, visiting family, traveling, biking, running or walking. It truly shines when it finds patterns in your data. It could be something simple, like telling you that on Mondays you usually enter the office at around 7:30 a.m., or something more complex, like how often you visit your parents (or when you last did, if it's been a while).

![Life Cycle][1]

After that initial year I joined the App Experience team working on new content and features for Sleep Cycle. The app started out as an alarm clock that analyses your sleep patterns and estimates your sleep stages. That data helps the app evaluate your sleep quality and choose a time to wake you when you are likely to feel more rested.

![Sleep Cycle][2]

Over my years there the app grew with new features, design updates and complete design overhauls. Beyond sleep tracking, it added snoring recordings, information on sleeping patterns, recorded stories to help users fall asleep and sleep notes for things that might affect their sleep. It also presented statistics on bedtimes, time taken to fall asleep, time in bed and estimated time asleep.

The app became a toolset for tackling sleep issues or simply keeping track of changes in sleep patterns and what might have caused them.

I later moved to the Growth team and worked on Android. That work covered marketing, analytics, remote configuration, reviews, conversion metrics, backend features and business-to-business services. Across my time at Sleep Cycle I used Objective-C, Swift, Kotlin, Java, SQL and Python.
