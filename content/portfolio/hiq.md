HiQ is a large consulting company that helps other companies digitalize their businesses or improve their existing digital services. My work there focused on native iOS development.
<!--more-->

This period includes my placement at HiQ while employed by New Minds from September 2014 to October 2015. After that placement I became a direct HiQ employee, staying until June 2017.

During this time I worked with around 10 clients of various sizes on equally varied projects. Since I am under a non-disclosure agreement I can't speak freely about these clients or their projects, but I am allowed to mention some by name: Mölnlycke Healthcare, Volvofinans Bank, Volvo, Parkeringsbolaget and Göta Lejon. The projects focused on mobile development, or in some cases the Internet of Things, with Objective-C and Swift as the primary languages.

For collaboration I used JIRA, Hansoft, Trello and Microsoft TFS. For distribution we mainly used TestFlight, Buddybuild or HockeyApp. Working closely with UX specialists and designers also brought me into contact with Zeplin, Sketch and InVision, although I did not create content in those tools.

My role was mostly frontend development, and in many cases I was the sole developer, since several projects had development cycles of a few weeks to a few months. The two longest projects each spanned about a year; I was a junior developer in one and a senior developer in the other.
